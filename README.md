<h2>🎯 What is CoderBar?</h2>
<p>CoderBar is a simple macOS application that lives in your menu bar and gives you control over Codex and Claude AI agents. You can monitor what these agents are doing, approve their actions, ask them questions, and jump back to previous steps — all without leaving your menu bar.</p>
<p>Think of it as a remote control for your AI coding assistant. Instead of switching windows, you can manage everything from one small icon at the top of your screen.</p>

<h2>📸 How It Works</h2>
<p>CoderBar sits quietly in your menu bar (the top bar on your Mac screen). When you click on the CoderBar icon, a dropdown appears showing:</p>
<ul>
<li>A list of active AI agents (Codex and Claude)</li>
<li>Their current status (thinking, waiting, done)</li>
<li>Buttons to approve, reject, or modify their actions</li>
<li>A text field to type custom instructions</li>
<li>A history timeline to jump back to earlier steps</li>
</ul>
<p>You never need to leave your current application or window. Everything happens right from your menu bar.</p>

<h2>🔧 Troubleshooting</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>App not opening?</strong> Make sure you're running macOS Big Sur (11) or later. If it still won't open, right-click the app in your Applications folder, choose <em>Open</em> from the menu, and confirm you want to open it (this is a one-time security step for apps not from the App Store).</li>
<li><strong>Codex or Claude not showing up?</strong> Make sure your AI agent app is running and you've granted CoderBar accessibility permissions in <strong>System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Menu bar icon missing?</strong> Check that CoderBar is running (look for it in your Process Explorer or Activity Monitor). If not, relaunch from Applications.</li>
<li><strong>Not receiving notifications?</strong> Enable notifications for CoderBar in <em>System Settings → Notifications</em>.</li>
</ul>
